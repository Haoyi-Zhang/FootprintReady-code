# External material

The repository source, generated synthetic cases and result data were created for
this investigation. No third-party benchmark, implementation, solver, or model
weights are included. Python is an external runtime, not bundled code.

Scholarly papers in the external-resource ledger were read through public web
interfaces to the extent stated in that ledger. They are cited, not redistributed.
Availability of a readable author copy is not treated as permission to relicense
or bundle it. In particular, the inspected WCEC author manuscript carries a
personal-use/non-redistribution notice.

The project-level paper package retains the supplied unmodified IEEEtran class
and bibliography style, whose own headers specify LPPL 1.3 and original authors.
Those publisher assets are not needed or included in this standalone repository.
